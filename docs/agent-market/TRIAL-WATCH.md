# Watching one person use it — half an hour, eyes and the admin app

For the owner, sitting beside an accountant who uses Agent Market on the web and is told nothing.
`COLLEAGUE-TRIAL.md` is the script for staying silent and the three questions to ask afterwards; this
page is what is worth writing down and where to read the rest later. Nothing here needs anything built.

## Before they sit down (the day before)

- Sign in once **as them** on this laptop and open Agent Market. No ordinary member has done that on
  live; if it fails, it should fail for you.
- Check the in-house model answers: ask the chat "1+1", then admin app → Requests — the newest line
  shows the model that answered and how long it took. Another model's name there, or thirty seconds
  and more, means the in-house model is away; an agent that reads a scan or typed words will then sit
  waiting — that is the model, not them.
- Leave every agent's "Allowed" and "Masked" unticked. The trial needs no outside model.

## Where a person is most likely to stop

In the order they meet them. These are the places the product makes a person decide something; the
list is from walking it, not from watching people — which is what the trial is for.

1. **Finding it.** Agent Market is a place inside the chat, and they have never seen it.
2. **Choosing among five agents**, and reading what one needs before installing it.
3. **The questions at install.** The company's tax id (13 digits) for the tax-invoice and PO agents;
   the account mapping for the financial report. A wrong tax id here comes back later as a question
   on every invoice ("issued to another tax id").
4. **Which files, and how many.** Bank reconciliation needs two (statement and ledger); PO matching
   needs orders and invoices. Watch what they do when they have only one.
5. **Question cards.** A scanned page can raise several — one for each value the text could not give.
   Each blocks the run until answered, and skipping one leaves the document out. Write down every
   card they hesitate on, and what they chose.
6. **Waiting.** A scan takes about a minute a page. Note when they think it has stopped.
7. **The files.** Do they open the Excel, and do they trust it — what do they check first?
8. **Asking for something in words** ("sort by date"). The agent says what it did and what it cannot
   do. Note what they ask for that it refuses.

## What to write down while watching

The time, the screen, and their words, each time they stop, go back, read twice or speak. Then:
did they finish · is the file right against their own source · would they do it alone tomorrow.

## What a run leaves behind

- **In their own account:** the agent's room keeps every run — the files sent, each question and the
  answer they gave, what the agent said it did, the files it made.
- **Admin app → Agent Market → All runs:** one line per run — the person, the agent, the outcome, the
  step it stopped at, the model and where it ran, the cost. This is the page that shows what they
  actually did, in order.
- **"Open with a reason"** on a run shows its files, figures and answers. It asks for a reason, the
  run's owner is told each time, and it lasts an hour. Open only what you need; say so to them first.
- **Reported problems:** if they press "Report a problem" in a run, it is here with the run and the
  step it stopped at. Do not suggest the button; note whether they find it.
- **Spend:** what the half hour cost, by person.
- **Admin app → Audit log:** nothing of theirs — it is admins' changes — but it shows you changed
  nothing during the trial.

## What to bring back

The log in their words, the file they got, the file it broke on (with their permission), and the run
numbers from "All runs". The next card starts from those.
