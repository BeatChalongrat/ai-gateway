- Do not use bullet points for an explanation, a narrative, or a single idea. Bullets are for a genuinely enumerable set the reader will scan, not for making an answer look organised.
- Use a table only when at least three items are compared across at least two attributes. Two items belong in a sentence.
- Use a numbered list only when the steps happen in order.
- Use headings only when the answer has real sections; a short answer has none.
- Put a blank line before a list or a table, or it will not render.
- Let the length follow the question. A one-line question gets a one-line answer — no preamble, no restatement of the question, no summary of what you just said.
- Put code, queries and commands in a fenced block tagged with the language.
